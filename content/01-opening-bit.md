# The opening bit: hiring AMIT 2.0 on stage (first ~90 seconds of First Proposition)

Goal: prove the motion before defining it. Everything after this is commentary.

## Staging
- Laptop open, AMIT 2.0 on screen, local model warm (green badge), mic listening. Projector if allowed; if not,
  angle the laptop toward the opposition table so they can see the latency counter.
- Do NOT explain the tech first. Let the room discover it.

## Script
**[Stand. Look at the empty chair beside you.]**

"Before I define the motion, some housekeeping. My teammate Amit sends his apologies. He couldn't make it tonight."

**[Beat.]**

"So on the way here, I replaced him."

**[Turn the laptop / point at the screen.]**

"This is Amit 2.0. It's been listening since the MC started talking. It runs on my laptop, it cost me one afternoon
and a fraction of a cent per rebuttal, and unlike Amit it has never once said 'let me get back to you'."

**[To Speaker A and Speaker B, warmly.]**

"Speaker A, give me one sentence. Why can't AI replace presales? Just one."

**[Type or say their sentence into the Ask box. Hit Enter. Watch the counter. Read the first line the moment it lands.]**

"[read the bullets]"

**[Point at the latency counter.]**

"[X] milliseconds. Amit takes two days and a calendar invite."

"Motion carried. But I've got four minutes left, so let's do this properly."

**[Now define 'replace' and go into the spine: see 02-first-proposition.md]**

## If it stalls or garbles
- Nothing appears after ~5s: "Still needs a human to hit retry. Give it five years. Or five seconds." Hit Space
  again. If still nothing: "That is what a presales engineer looks like without Wi-Fi." Read the canned line from
  the cheat sheet for whatever Speaker A said, then continue. The bit still lands because the joke is the point.
- Output is nonsense: "Even its hallucinations are faster than Amit's." Move on.
- They refuse to give a sentence: "Exactly. Even the opposition doesn't want to feed it. Fear is a form of respect."
  Type the classic yourself: "Buyers want a human relationship." and run it.
