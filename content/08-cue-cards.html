HOW TO USEBackup deck

Laptop dies, Wi-Fi dies, or you just want paper. This deck covers the whole night.

Cards 2-8: your first speech, scripted. Read them.

Card 9: listen card for their first speaker. Tick the angle they take.

Cards 10-19: second speech. Bridge (10), hand-over (11), ONE rebuttal card matching their angle (12-17), your points (18) and close (19).

Card 20: listen card for their second speaker.

Cards 21-29: summary. Frame (21), concessions (22), hand-over (23), ONE closing card per angle (24-28), final close (29).

Cards 30-34: their lines and yours (30-32), the six facts (33), failure lines (34).

Print two per sheet on A4 landscape. Number order is speaking order.
