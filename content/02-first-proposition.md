**[Turn the screen towards the room.]**

## [1:00-1:45] What "replace" means

"The motion says AI will replace us in five years. Let me pin down 'replace', because the opposition is going to spend ten minutes living inside that word."

**"Replaced means the buyer reaches a decision without needing a human presales engineer in the room."**

"It doesn't mean zero headcount. Somewhere in this city there is still a man who fixes typewriters, and good for him. But when Speaker A tells you there'll still be a human in the loop, hear what it means for the ten of us who used to run the demos: two stay behind to watch the machine run them. That isn't a profession surviving. That's a profession with two witnesses."

## [1:45-2:45] What we actually do all day

"Let's be honest about the job. We're among friends and nobody from procurement is here."

- "Discovery calls. The AI note-taker in your last call listened better than you did, and had the follow-up drafted before you found your charger."
- "Demos. Generated per prospect now, and watched at one-and-a-half speed because nobody wants to sit through 'and if I just click here'."
- "RFPs and security questionnaires. Hands up if you still fill one in by hand. Nobody. That pillar fell two years ago and we threw a party."
- "POCs. An agent provisions the trial, loads the sample data and answers the tickets while we're asleep."
- "Objection handling. The documentation plus a model beats 'let me check with product' every time, because the model actually checked."

"Five pillars, and every one already has a product. What we do is stand between those products and glue them together with our calendars, and the glue is always the first thing automated."

## [2:45-3:15] What they'll say next

"Speaker A and Speaker B are about to tell you that fewer people per deal isn't the same as no people in the profession. Let me save them the trouble: they're right, and it doesn't help them. Singapore had bus conductors until the eighties, somebody on the 174 with a ticket punch. There are still bus conductors somewhere in the world, and not one person in this room is planning to become one. The motion says replace us. Us. The people in this room."

## [3:15-3:50] Five years is a long time

"They'll say five years is too soon. Look backwards five years instead. September 2021: GPT-3 was a party trick that could just about write a limerick. If I'd told you then that a laptop would rebut you live, in one second, in your own words, you'd have laughed. In ten minutes you get to watch it. Don't judge this motion by what the thing does today. Judge it by how far it came in five years, then go the same distance again."

## [3:50-4:30] Buyers never wanted us

"Here's the uncomfortable one. Buyers never wanted us. They wanted the answer, and for twenty years we were the slowest way to get it."

"Gartner asked 646 B2B buyers this year. **Sixty-seven percent said they'd prefer a buying experience with no rep at all.** Not fewer meetings. None. Think about the last deal you won and ask whether the buyer picked you because they liked the SE, or because the SE answered the question. Answers are free now. So what exactly is left to sell?"
