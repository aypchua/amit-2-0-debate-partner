# Cheat sheet: their line -> your line
Print this. One page. Lines marked with -> are also what the **Panic** button shows on screen.

## Definitions (say these early, repeat them often)
- **Replace** = the buyer reaches a decision without needing a human presales engineer in the room.
- Not "zero headcount". Singapore had bus conductors until the eighties. Nobody here is planning to be one.
- If they say "some humans will remain" or "human in the loop", they have conceded. Say so, out loud, every time.

## Pre-empted opposition lines
- **"AI hallucinates."** -> So do SEs. We call it "roadmap".
- **"Complex enterprise deals need humans."** -> Complexity is a symptom of humans. Remove the humans, remove the complexity.
- **"AI can't read the room."** -> The room is a Zoom call with cameras off. There is no room.
- **"Regulated industries, security, compliance."** -> The security questionnaire was the first thing we automated. Ask anyone here who still fills one in by hand.
- **"Trust is built over years and dinners."** -> Buyers trust Google Maps over a local. They tell chatbots things they won't tell their doctor.
- **"Relationships close deals."** -> Procurement portals close deals. Relationships got you the meeting the AI already booked.
- **"You're an SE arguing yourself out of a job."** -> I'm arguing I'll be the one hitting retry. For now.
- **"That's a party trick, the AI isn't really debating."** -> You have spent five minutes rebutting it.
- **"AI will make us more productive, not redundant."** -> "More productive" means fewer of us per deal. That is the definition of replacement, said politely.
- **"Buyers want a human relationship."** -> Gartner, this year: 67% of B2B buyers prefer no rep at all. They want the answer. We were the slowest way to get it.
- **"Gartner says 69% of buyers validate AI insights with a rep."** -> Same buyers, same survey window: 67% want no rep. Our surviving job, in Gartner's words, is checking the AI's homework. Fact-checker for the robot is not a career, it's a countdown.
- **"Five years is too soon."** -> Five years ago GPT-3 was a party trick. You are standing next to what it became.
- **"Someone has to be accountable when it breaks."** -> Accountability is a contract clause, not a job title. Legal already automated that too.
- **"Discovery needs empathy."** -> Discovery needs listening. The AI note-taker in your last call listened better than you did.
- **"Demos need to be tailored live."** -> Tailored demos are generated per prospect in seconds now. You tailor by clicking through the same slides.
- **"Customers pay for expertise."** -> Customers pay for outcomes. Expertise is a search away, and it doesn't take Fridays off.
- **"There's a human in the loop."** -> "Human in the loop" is what the loop says right before it closes.

## If the demo dies on stage
- **Stall:** "Still needs a human to hit retry. Give it five years. Or five seconds."
- **Garbage output:** "Even its hallucinations are faster than Amit's."
- **Nothing at all:** "That, ladies and gentlemen, is what a presales engineer looks like without Wi-Fi." Then read from paper.

## The flips (turn their best points into ours)
- Every "still needs a human" story describes a **shrinking** role. Ten SEs become two supervisors. Count the chairs.
- Every "it's not good enough yet" concedes the direction and argues only the date. We only need the date.
- Every "buyers love us" claim: ask when a buyer last chose a vendor because they liked the SE.

## Closing lines to keep in the pocket
- "The opposition has argued, passionately, that they are irreplaceable. They did it in the exact words my laptop predicted."
- "I came here one person short. I leave with the motion carried and my teammate never once said 'let me get back to you'."
