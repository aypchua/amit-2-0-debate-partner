## The one-screen flow (current plan; Show mode is retired)
1. Before start: `./app/run.sh`, green "local: warm". Mic check: **Listen**, say a sentence, see it appear, then **Discard** (empties without archiving). Do not leave Listen on during the MC or your own speech.
2. Your first speech: press **Script 1**; it appears paged in the big pane. Arrow keys or click to turn pages.
3. Their first speaker: **Clear**, then **Listen**. Watch the transcript and the number chips.
4. On their "thank you": **Space** (STOP & WRITE SPEECH 2). Your whole second speech is written live from what they said plus our prepared ideas. Stand when page 1 is full; read from the screen, turning pages. Open with: "Everything I am about to say was written by Amit 2.0 in the seconds since Speaker A sat down."
5. Their second speaker: **Clear** (this archives their first speech into memory), then **Listen**.
6. On their "thank you": **STOP & WRITE SUMMARY**. Your whole three-minute summary, from both speeches and what Amit already said. Read it.
7. **Reset** only between rehearsals or before the real thing. Never during the debate.
Clear archives (use only between opposition speakers). Discard empties without archiving (mic check, MC chatter, accidental capture). Reset wipes everything.
Short options if you prefer: **Rebut only** gives the 70-second version; **Steelman & flip** the concede-then-steal version.

## Driving the screen (Show mode, retired but still works with S)
- Press **S** for Show mode. **Right/Left arrow** moves between slots. Entering an opposition slot starts Listen; entering your slot stops it.
- **Space** = STOP & REBUT, use it the instant they say "thank you", then arrow right to your speech. The rebuttal appears inside your script where [[AMIT]] sits.
- **Tab** shows the full-screen rebuttal if you want it big. **P** = Panic lines.

## Debate memory
- Every opposition transcript is archived when you arrow out of its slot. Every Amit passage is kept with its slot.
- Slot 3 rebuttal sees the 1st Opposition speech. Slot 5 summary sees BOTH opposition speeches and everything Amit already said, and is told to count concessions and not repeat itself. Space in slot 4 or 6 produces a summary, not a plain rebuttal.
- **Reset** (red, asks to confirm) starts a new debate: wipes memory, transcript and panes. Use it between rehearsal runs. Clear never wipes memory; it archives.
- Memory works outside Show mode too: Clear archives the current speech instead of discarding it, and the Summary button next to STOP produces the closing passage from everything archived. Show mode just does the archiving for you when you arrow between slots.

## Rules for the night
- Andrew never reads more than 5 bullets in a row. Alternate: your voice, then "and Amit adds...", then yours.
- Say "Amit 2.0 says" before reading AI lines. The attribution IS the argument.
- Every time they say "human in the loop" or "some humans will remain", stop and say: "Concession noted."
- Keep the definition on the table: replaced = buyer decides without needing us in the room.
- Speak slower than you think. 5 minutes is ~650 words. Scripts are sized to that.

## Timing marks (5-min speeches)
- 0:00-1:30 opening bit / bridge
- 1:30-4:00 body (3-4 points max)
- 4:00-4:45 the flip or AMIT lines
- 4:45-5:00 closing line, sit down on the laugh
